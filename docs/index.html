    League

    League CP limits

    Little League is capped at 500 CP, Great at 1,500 CP, and Ultra at 2,500 CP. Master League has no CP cap.

    The selected League controls the CP ceiling. A suggested League shown with a Cup is a recommendation only and never changes your selection.

    Cup format and eligibility

    Cup restrictions apply to your selected League and do not change its CP ceiling. Some eligibility rules cannot be represented in Pokémon GO search and need a manual check.

    Include mutators

    With a Cup selected, leave this unchecked to use only the selected League CP limit and the Cup's static restrictions. Random mutators are omitted, and their settings are hidden.

    Check it to restore random mutator generation. Open League keeps the existing randomiser active even when this box is unchecked. Its state is preserved when you change Cups or Leagues.

    Additional Rules

    Additional Rules help

    Exclude Pokémon by name or Pokédex number, or add a Pokémon GO search term as a removable rule chip. Press Enter to add a selected Pokémon or valid search term.

    Pokémon GO search syntax

    Enter a Pokémon GO search expression. Use & for AND, commas for OR, and ! to exclude a term.

    Example: !shadow&@water excludes Shadow Pokémon and requires a Water-type move. fire,water matches either type.

    The expression is combined with the generated filter using Pokémon GO AND/OR precedence.
